## A record we can build on (accomplishments)

### 1. Building more space for students

Together, we've invested in the future of Niagara Falls students and families. We built the brand-new **Thundering Heights Elementary School**, adding **608 student spaces** and **49 child care spaces** for infants and youth. We expanded **Forestview Elementary School** with eight new classrooms, a new gymnasium, and additional child care spaces. We also have a proposal before the Ministry of Education to expand **Riverview Elementary School**, adding **10 new classrooms** and **49 child care spaces**. This summer, we also opened a brand-new **culinary program at Westlane Secondary School**, strengthening our investments in skilled trades and hands-on learning.

**Instagram (Riverview / Chippawa):** DSBN submitted a proposal to the Ministry of Education to build 10 new classrooms at Riverview Public School in Chippawa, plus a new daycare centre with space for 49 infants and children.  
Post: https://www.instagram.com/p/DchC1grOSLe/

### 2. Helping students succeed

Together, we've continued to raise the bar for student achievement. Our students have made gains in **EQAO results, literacy, and graduation rates**, with DSBN students regularly **outperforming provincial averages by as much as 15%**.

### 3. Improving classroom support

Student success means more than academic achievement — it means making sure every student feels welcome, supported, and able to succeed. Together, we've expanded supports by hiring more **social workers and youth workers** and strengthening our Special Education teams, including the addition of **23 new Education Assistants** this school year. We've also expanded our focus on **mental health and well-being**, including introducing mental health modules into the **Grade 10 curriculum**.

### 4. Creating stronger connections

Our schools are strongest when they are connected to the communities they serve. Throughout my terms as Trustee, I've worked to strengthen that connection through service on the **Parent Involvement Committee** and **Special Education Advisory Committee**, as well as through my role as **Chair of the Equity and Inclusion Committee**.

## What we'll keep working for (priorities)

### 1. Tackle school overcrowding

When classrooms and schools are overcrowded, students don't get the individual attention and support they deserve. I'll continue advocating for new capital investments in Niagara Falls, including **two new schools in South Niagara Falls**, to address enrollment growth, reduce overcrowding and create better learning environments for students and educators.

### 2. Building safer schools

Every student and every education worker deserves to feel safe at school. We are seeing increasing concerns about violence and safety in our classrooms. I'll continue advocating for immediate investments in **frontline staffing, mental health supports, Special Education** and other student services.

**Related advocacy (Instagram):** 77% of educators report witnessing or experiencing violence at school; $6.5 billion cut from public education since 2018; 67,000 students waiting for autism supports.  
Post: https://www.instagram.com/p/Dct9cjcj7Wv/

### 3. Improving special education

Every student deserves the support they need to thrive in the classroom. Families are facing long waits for assessments and schools are dealing with shortages of Education Assistants and specialized staff. I'll continue working with the Board and advocating to the provincial government for the staff, resources and funding necessary to ensure students can learn and participate fully in their classrooms.

### 4. Fix our aging schools

During the **2026–27 school year**, the DSBN is investing **$124 million** in major renovations, system upgrades and new school construction, but significant infrastructure needs remain. I'll continue advocating for sustained provincial capital funding to address the repair backlog and modernize aging schools.

## Hosting notes

This is a **static HTML/CSS/JS** site with no build step. The campaign hosts on their own GitHub or web provider.

**To go live on GitHub Pages (campaign team):**
1. Push repo to GitHub
2. Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`
3. Add custom domain `shannonmitchellfortrustee.com` and configure DNS
4. Add a `CNAME` file containing `shannonmitchellfortrustee.com` if using custom domain

**Local preview:**
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```
